# Stakeholder Communication Standard

## 1. One-sentence explanation

We identify players who contribute more than comparable players would normally be expected to contribute in difficult T20 match situations, while checking that the evidence is sufficiently large and consistent.

## 2. Explain pressure without jargon

Pressure is not just the last five overs. It depends on how difficult the match situation is:
- how many runs are needed
- how many balls remain
- how many wickets remain
- how quickly the team must score
- whether the innings is behind or ahead of a reasonable scoring expectation

## 3. Explain the score

Use a four-part player card:

1. **Pressure contribution:** how much value the player added above expectation.
2. **Opportunity:** how many pressure innings/overs and matches were observed.
3. **Reliability:** whether the performance was repeated or driven by one exceptional event.
4. **Confidence:** how uncertain the estimate is.

Avoid presenting a score without its denominator and uncertainty.

## 4. Example wording (illustrative only)

"Player A ranks highly because, across 18 pressure innings, they scored above the expected contribution for batters in comparable roles and match states. Their result is supported across several matches, rather than one exceptional innings. The estimate is still uncertain because the sample is limited."

Do not populate this example with invented real player statistics.

## 5. Visuals

Recommended:
- pressure distribution by innings phase
- observed vs expected contribution
- player opportunity count vs adjusted rating
- confidence intervals / uncertainty bands
- rank stability across alternative pressure definitions
- one annotated delivery/innings case study

## 6. Words to avoid

Do not claim:
- "mentally strongest player"
- "clutch gene"
- "guaranteed match-winner"
- "best player" without defining the scope
- "predicts success" without out-of-sample validation

Prefer:
- "performed above expectation in the defined pressure situations"
- "pressure-adjusted contribution"
- "evidence strength"
- "estimated reliability"
- "ranking stability"

## 7. Required explanation for each Top-10 selection

For every selected player, report:
- rank and rating
- pressure opportunities and distinct matches
- role/phase profile
- observed contribution vs expected contribution
- strongest evidence and main limitation
- uncertainty interval or confidence category
- whether the rank is stable under alternative specifications
